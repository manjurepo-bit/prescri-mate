# PresciMate 💊

**PresciMate** 💊 — an app that reads a photo of a handwritten prescription and explains it back to you in plain language, in your own Indian language (Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, and 9 others).

## 💡 Why?
In a lot of small towns/villages in India, a prescription comes in English with brand names nobody recognizes, and there's no one around to explain what it actually means. Wanted to fix that.

## ✨ What it does
* **Upload or photograph a prescription**: Supports direct file upload or mobile camera integration.
* **Sign up first**: Secure authentication to ensure everything stays private and custom to your account.
* **Get it explained in your own language**: Extracts active ingredients, explanation of purpose, dosage instructions, and warnings in the native script of 12 Indian languages.
* **Flags drug-drug interactions**: Automatically checks if prescribed medicines have potential moderate or major adverse interactions.
* **Download explanation as a PDF**: Instantly generates clean, portable reports formatted with Indian language fonts.
* **Doctor-First Policy**: Always points patients back to their doctor, prioritizing safety and professional medical advice.
