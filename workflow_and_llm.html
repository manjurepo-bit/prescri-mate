🧠 How DoseMate Works & The LLM Technology

DoseMate translates hard-to-read medical prescriptions into clear, regional guides through 6 key engineering steps:

  1. Step 1: Visual Reading (Google Gemini LLM)
    We use the state-of-the-art Gemini 3.5 Flash vision model. When you upload a photo, the LLM analyzes the pixels directly to recognize messy, handwritten characters (OCR) that traditional scanners miss.
  2. Step 2: Medical Details Extraction (Structured Output)
    The Gemini LLM uses strict Pydantic schemas to ensure it extracts medicines without hallucinating. It separates each medicine's Brand Name, Active Chemical Ingredient, Dosage, and detailed Health Benefits.
  3. Step 3: Drug Interaction Safety Check (NetworkX Graph)
    We pass the extracted chemical ingredients into a NetworkX Graph Database. The graph instantly checks for safety issues (e.g., if you are prescribed two blood thinners like Aspirin and Warfarin at the same time, it flags a Major warning).
  4. Step 4: Smart Translation (Mixed-Script Engine)
    We translate the explanations into your chosen Indian language. To keep it safe, the translation engine translates the instructions into local script (like Hindi Devanagari) but preserves the brand/generic names in English script in brackets so you can match the packaging.
  5. Step 5: Secure Qdrant Database Storage
    Your prescription details are converted into numerical vector coordinates (embeddings) and stored in a private Qdrant Vector Database. This allows you to search your history instantly using natural search queries.
  6. Step 6: Printable PDF Compilation (ReportLab)
    On demand, the app builds a multi-page PDF report. It dynamically injects the correct Google Noto font file to support regional characters alongside English characters without formatting issues.

🔬 Always consult your doctor before modifying your medicine intake. DoseMate is an AI assistant, not a clinical provider.
